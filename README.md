# 80 Days to Ashes
Contains the files for the game "80 Days to Ashes", created for the project "thirdspace" by HackClub. Play it here: [LINK HERE]

## Summary
### I. Lore
  You and your significant other lives in a small humble village, 30 miles away from the nearest city bastion. One day, your village chieftain orders you and some other men to go into the city to buy preparation supplies for autumn and winter. You and your companions, after readying up your animals, wave your families good bye, as you depart for your 2-day journey over forests, hills, and rivers. After arriving in the city, you and your companions rested and enjoyed the pleasantries of the city. After 2 more days, you and your companions finally departed from the city with all the goods your village needed. 
  
  After only 3 miles of travel, you encounter one of your village’s scouts. Their clothes are torn and muddy, it seems as if they ran all the way from your village to where they stand panting now. After the scout catches their breath, they inform you of what happened back in the village. He explains that on the third night after they left, the Iron Chain, an infamous guild, raided your village, killing all who fought back and enslaving all those who surrendered. He was only able to escape because he was at the right place at the right time. You and your companion froze at what you’ve just heard, the night where you were all out drinking and resting, was the night that your village got attacked. Before you could fully grasp the severity of the situation, arrows came flying through the air, killing the scout and one of your companions. It turns out the scout wasn’t able to escape after all. Because of the commotion, the horse you were riding started to jump in fear and ran back to where you came from. 
  
  After you arrive back at the city, you sit in one of the benches near the market place. With your village wiped out, your family gone, and your companions killed, you pledge to yourself that you will avenge them and destroy that wretched guild once and for all.
  
  During your quest to get stronger in order to get your revenge, you learn about powerful artifacts scattered around the continent, collecting them would result in power levels strong enough to wipe the guild. Along with this, you also learn that the guild often starts a black market auction, before shipping their victims to faraway lands overseas. This gives you an opportunity to strike at the enemy or to buy your family out yourself. This auction is held twice per year, once at the start of spring, and another at the last day of summer. This gives you around 80 more days before an auction will take place.


### II. Gameplay Components
- **SAVING SYSTEM:** Ability to have different save files (CURRENTLY BEING WORKED ON)
- **POINT AND CLICK:** Point-and-click dungeon exploration
- **TURN BASED RPG** Contains elements of turn-based combat

We definitely want to polish these systems and include more stuff in the future! But this was the extent of our abilities given the limited time we have, as busy students and only a few amateur programmers :]
